# Hiwaya Product Roadmap

This document outlines completed milestones, active developments, and planned future capabilities for the **Hiwaya** platform.

---

## 🏁 Phase 1: Core Foundation & Quest Architecture (Completed ✅)

- [x] **Interactive Adventure Quest Map**: Dynamic multi-step node progression with prerequisite validation and status badges.
- [x] **3D/Animated AI Companions**: 4 unique interactive mascots (Sparky, Luna, Pip, Barnaby) with dynamic motivational dialogues and animation states.
- [x] **Procedural Audio Engine**: Web Audio API synthesizer for tactile UI sound effects (chimes, pops, fanfares, celebration cheers).
- [x] **Multi-Course Content Library**: Step-by-step masterclass courses covering Origami, Crochet, Painting, STEM Rocketry, Pottery, Arabic Calligraphy, and Junior Finance.
- [x] **Digital Drawing Pad Modal**: In-app canvas tool enabling learners to draw, color, and save artwork directly on-screen.
- [x] **Milestone Celebrations**: Canvas confetti, animated badge unlocks, and shareable achievement certificate cards.

---

## 🛡️ Phase 2: Role Separation & Parental Safety (Completed ✅)

- [x] **Triple-Role Access Model**: Dedicated support for Parents, Child Learners (6–12), and Teen Makers (13+).
- [x] **PIN-Protected Parent Hub**: 4-digit security PIN modal securing analytics, child settings, and subscription management.
- [x] **Parent Moderation Queue**: Approval workflow for child submissions prior to public showcase in the community gallery.
- [x] **Teen Maker Experience**: Autonomous workflow for 13+ learners featuring direct publishing and dedicated maker studio statistics.
- [x] **Screen-Time & Category Management**: Configurable daily learning time allowances and hobby subject toggles.
- [x] **Robust Authentication & Input Validation**: Validated email and 6-character password rules on both login and onboarding steps.

---

## 💎 Phase 3: Monetization & Partner Rewards (Completed ✅)

- [x] **Course Gating & Strict Paywall**: Free starter access for Origami & Crochet, with full paywall protection across locked course videos, checklists, and AI evaluators.
- [x] **Hiwaya Premium & Hobby Addon Plans**: Monthly/Annual tier subscriptions and individual hobby course unlock options (QAR currency formatting).
- [x] **Tangible Partner Vouchers**: Milestone reward redemption for verified brand partners (Alif Stories, Jarir Bookstore, Virgin Megastore, Hobby Haven).
- [x] **Community Showcase Gallery**: Filterable public feed with creator avatars, project badges, and peer praise reactions.

---

## 🚀 Phase 4: Active Enhancements (In Progress 🔄)

- [ ] **Multi-Language Support (Arabic & English)**: Full RTL layout support and bilingual companion voice dialogues for regional accessibility in Qatar and the GCC.
- [ ] **Physical Craft Kit Delivery Integration**: Linking digital courses with delivered monthly physical craft supply boxes.
- [ ] **Enhanced AI Vision Scorer**: Expanding the Google Gemini multimodal evaluation pipeline for fine-grained fold precision and color balance feedback.
- [ ] **Family Multi-Profile Switching**: Quick switcher allowing parents to manage multiple siblings under a single parent account.

---

## 🔮 Phase 5: Long-Term Vision & Planned Features (Planned 📋)

- [ ] **Live Interactive Maker Workshops**: Scheduled live video sessions with verified local craft educators and artisans.
- [ ] **Augmented Reality (AR) Step Guidance**: AR camera overlay demonstrating 3D fold angles and crochet stitch loops in real-time.
- [ ] **Offline Quest Downloads**: Progressive Web App (PWA) caching allowing learners to follow craft instructions without active Wi-Fi.
- [ ] **School & Classroom Portals**: Group challenge leaderboards and teacher oversight dashboards for educational institutions.
- [ ] **Expanded GCC Partner Network**: Additional partnerships with museums, youth centers, and science clubs across the region.
